How this was built

Data. All 6,460 Premier League results 2009/10–2025/26, plus 2002/03–2008/09 for City's history, from the football-data.co.uk results archive. Tables use points, then goal difference, then goals scored. Points deductions are applied in every scenario: Portsmouth −9 (2009/10), Everton −8 and Nottingham Forest −4 (2023/24).

Without City. City's 38 matches are deleted and the remaining 342 results are tabulated as they stand.

Pre-takeover City. A Poisson team-strength model (attack, defence, home advantage) is fitted to each season's 342 non-City matches to rate every opponent. City keep their average 2002–08 balance of attack to defence, scaled so that their expected points across the real fixture list match the 2002–08 points trend line for that season. Each fixture is then simulated 20,000 times. The table shows expected totals; title, top-four and relegation odds come from the simulations, with everything except City's games held at the real results.

Limits. The other 342 results are fixed, so knock-on effects are ignored: transfers, managers, fatigue and the 2011/12 final day all stay as they were. A linear trend over six seasons is a modest projection, and City's actual 2008/09 (50 pts, 10th) sits right on it. Carried forward to 2025/26 it gives about 56 points, so the further out the season, the more this is a thought experiment.

Source: football-data.co.uk Premier League results. Model and simulations computed offline; nothing on this page calls out to the network.
